Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 August 2026

LYNS helps people discover events and things to do around Stellenbosch. This policy explains what information we collect and why.

If you're just browsing

You do not need an account to use LYNS. We do not ask for your name, email, or any personal details, and we do not track you across other apps or websites.

Events you save are stored only on your own device (in your browser's local storage). They are never sent to us. Clearing your browser data removes them.

If you post events as an organiser

To list events you sign in with your email address (via a one-time link — we never see or store a password). When you set up an organiser profile we collect:

  • Your name or venue name
  • Your email address
  • Your phone number and Instagram handle, if you choose to add them
  • A short description of the events you run, and an optional logo or photo

We use this only to verify that you are a real organiser and to contact you about your submissions. Event details and cover images you submit are shown publicly in the app.

Who processes this data

  • Supabase — database, sign-in, and image storage.
  • Vercel — website hosting.
  • Google — sends the sign-in emails, and serves the app's fonts.

We do not sell your data or share it with advertisers. LYNS does not currently show ads.

Keeping and deleting data

Organiser details are kept while your account is active. To delete your account and data, email us and we'll remove it.

Children

LYNS is intended for a general audience of students and adults. It is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes we'll update the date above and, for anything significant, let organisers know by email.
