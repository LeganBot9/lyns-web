Delete your account and data

Last updated: 10 September 2026

If you only browse LYNS

You don't have an account and we hold no data about you. Events you save are stored only on your own device — clear your browser data, or delete the app, and they're gone. There is nothing for us to delete.

If you have an organiser account

Email lynsStellie@gmail.com from the email address on your organiser account, with the subject "Delete my LYNS account".

What gets deleted

  • Your organiser profile — name, email, phone, Instagram handle, description, and logo
  • Your sign-in credentials
  • Any events you submitted that are not currently live

What we may keep

  • Events of yours that are already published stay listed until they have passed or you ask us to remove them too, so the public listing isn't broken mid-run.
  • Basic records we're required to keep for legal or security reasons (kept no longer than needed, then deleted).

We action deletion requests within 30 days and email you to confirm when it's done.

Contact

Any questions: lynsStellie@gmail.com. See also our Privacy Policy.
